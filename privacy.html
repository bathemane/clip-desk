Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Clip Desk is a personal tool. Only its owner uses it, with the owner's own accounts.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and read the channel name and video view counts.
  • TikTok: basic profile info (display name), permission to send videos to the account's drafts, and the list of the account's public videos to read view counts.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels and read their view counts.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and view counts are stored only on the owner's computer, in Clip Desk's local data folder. They are used only to post the owner's approved clips and to show the owner how those clips perform. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect any account in Clip Desk, which deletes its token, and can revoke access at any time from the Google, TikTok or Meta account settings.
